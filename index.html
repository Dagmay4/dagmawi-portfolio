Who I am

I'm a Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning specialist who has spent over six years working at the intersection of field operations and strategic insight — at organizations like FAO, IIE, and GIZ, across Ethiopia and beyond.

My edge is bridging the gap between on-the-ground implementation and evidence-based decision-making. Whether that means designing a digital data collection system for desert locust response, leading end-of-project evaluations, or training teams on PMEL frameworks, I deliver work that makes programs more accountable and more impactful.

I've also built a track record as a trainer — delivering M&E courses to institutions and individuals across sectors, including a recent PMEL training for Cheshire Ethiopia and Liliane Foundation.
